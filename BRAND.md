# Eidomira

**Pronunciation:** eye-doh-MEER-ah  
**Product descriptor:** Live neural identity  
**Working tagline:** Be present. Appear differently.

## Name rationale

`Eidomira` combines *eidos*—form, appearance, or identity—with *mira*—look, wonder, and attention. It is coined rather than descriptive, allowing the brand to expand beyond face swapping into live identity, style, and presence tools.

## Icon rationale

The icon uses two interlocking luminous forms. At large sizes they suggest layered identity and motion; at small sizes the negative space reads as both a profile and camera aperture. Violet represents transformation, while cyan represents live signal and precision.

## Core usage

- Primary wordmark: **EIDOMIRA**
- Product variant: **EIDOMIRA LIVE**
- API name: **Eidomira Live API**
- Icon may appear without the wordmark in app launchers and browser favicons.
