Boost lab

The sub-pixel resolution recovery from app/boost.py, running in your browser on your device, with its own timings. Point a camera at something detailed and watch the difference between one pass and four.

No swap weights here. There is no inswapper file on this server, so this page cannot and does not perform a face swap. It runs a small stand-in model through the same runtime a real deployment would use, and measures the runtime and the boost on your device. A real swapper is ~554 MB and far slower than the stand-in — treat the timings as the floor, not the estimate.

Output

Press Start to measure. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is recorded, and the camera stops the moment you press Stop.

The square is the aligned crop region. At 1× you are seeing what a 128-pixel model can carry; at 2× and 3× the phases are sampled between those pixels and interleaved.
